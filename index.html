4. Additional Variables to Track

  • Distribution completion: percentage of printed flyers actually delivered.
  • Audience match: how closely the location and audience fit the offer.
  • QR scan rate: scans divided by flyers or posters distributed.
  • Landing-page conversion: visitors who complete a purchase.
  • Payment processing fees: card, PayPal and cryptocurrency service fees.
  • Refunds and chargebacks: subtract losses from expected proceeds.
  • Fulfillment costs: include the actual cost of promised rewards.
  • Repeat purchases: track returning customers separately.
  • Time and labor: value the hours spent printing and distributing.
  • Campaign source codes: use a unique URL or QR code for each location or flyer.

5. Top 10 Other Sales Methods

  1. Landing page: explain the offer, pricing, conditions and next step.
  2. Email newsletter: contact people who opted in and provide an unsubscribe option.
  3. Social media videos: demonstrate the product and link to the official page.
  4. Referral program: reward verified referrals with transparent terms.
  5. Local business partnerships: work with relevant shops and event organizers.
  6. Events and market stalls: use a QR code and a clear explanation of the offer.
  7. Search engine optimization (SEO): publish useful pages that answer customer questions.
  8. Paid advertising: test small budgets and measure customer acquisition costs.
  9. Affiliate partners: use tracked links and disclose commissions.
  10. Educational demonstrations: explain how the product works, risks, pricing and support.

If Pizza Coin is marketed as a cryptoasset or investment, avoid guaranteed-return claims. Check applicable advertising, consumer-protection, securities, privacy and anti-spam rules.
